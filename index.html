Welcome to Manas

A destination where every moment becomes a memory.

Set in the green folds of the Sahyadri hills at Talegaon, Igatpuri, Manas Lifestyle Resort is where city weekends slow down — mist on the lawns in the monsoon, long lunches, a pool that glows after dark.

Stay in a garden cottage, a pool-view suite or a private pool villa; spend the day between the swimming pool, the rain dance and the games zone; dine at Baithak, Café Fusion and the Sweet Poison bar lounge. And when life calls for a celebration, our ballroom and lawns host everything from intimate engagements to weddings of 1,200 guests. A unit of Ashoka Group.

Good to know

Before you visit

Quick answers to what guests ask most. More on our FAQ page.

Where is Manas Lifestyle Resort?

On the Mumbai–Agra Highway at Talegaon, Igatpuri, in Nashik district, Maharashtra — about 120 km (roughly 2.5 hours) from Mumbai and 40 km (about 40 minutes) from Nashik. Directions and nearby places.

What types of accommodation are available?

Luxury Cottages, Premium, Urban and Luxe Rooms, Junior, Studio, Signature and Harmony Suites, a Suite with a separate living room, and private pool villas from 4 to 7 bedrooms. Compare all stays.

Is the resort good for families with children?

Yes. There is a swimming pool with different depths, a rain dance, an arcade, indoor and outdoor games, magic shows and pottery, plus family rooms with an extra bed.

Can I host a wedding or corporate event here?

Yes. The pillarless Mist Ballroom hosts up to 500 guests reception-style and the Royal Lawn up to 1,200. See weddings and corporate events.

How do I get the best rate?

Book directly on this website or call reservations on +91 98500 02234. Current packages are listed on the offers page.
