Best Resorts Near Nashik – Discover Luxury & Nature at Manas Lifestyle Resort in Igatpuri

Looking for the best resort near Nashik that combines luxury, comfort, and breathtaking natural surroundings? Tucked away in the scenic Sahyadri ranges, Manas Lifestyle Resort in Igatpuri Nashik is a dream destination for families, couples, and leisure travelers. Igatpuri, a hill station just 2 hours from Mumbai, is known for its lush green landscapes, misty mountains, and peaceful atmosphere—making it one of the most popular weekend getaways from Mumbai and Pune.

With world-class amenities, spacious villas, organic dining options, and warm hospitality, Manas Lifestyle Resort has earned its reputation as one of the best resorts in Igatpuri. Whether you’re planning a romantic escape with your partner, a family vacation, or a destination wedding, this luxury resort in Igatpuri has everything you need for an unforgettable stay.

Why Manas Lifestyle Resort is the Best Resort in Igatpuri Nashik

When you’re searching for a resort in Igatpuri Nashik, you want more than just accommodation—you want an experience. Manas Lifestyle Resort goes beyond expectations by offering a blend of natural beauty and modern luxury.

Families love the spacious cottages and family suites that allow everyone to stay together while enjoying complete privacy. The resort offers plenty of kid-friendly activities, open lawns, and safe play areas. For couples, private villas and romantic setups make it a resort near Nashik for couples that sparks unforgettable moments.

Unlike regular hotels, Manas Lifestyle Resort focuses on creating holistic experiences. From early morning yoga sessions in the gardens to evenings spent by the poolside, every detail is designed to help guests unwind. It’s not just the best resort near Nashik—it’s a place where you reconnect with yourself, your loved ones, and nature.

Luxury Resorts in Igatpuri – Redefining Comfort & Elegance

Among the many luxury resorts in Igatpuri, Manas Lifestyle Resort stands out for its world-class amenities and design. The property features premium villas with wooden interiors, elegant suites, and cozy cottages that bring a sense of warmth and sophistication. Each room is equipped with modern facilities while maintaining a rustic charm that blends with Igatpuri’s natural surroundings.

Guests can indulge in fine dining with multiple restaurants serving Indian, Chinese, and Continental delicacies. If you’re a wellness enthusiast, the spa and wellness center provides rejuvenating therapies and massages to melt away stress. For adventure seekers, the resort arranges nature treks, waterfall visits, and vineyard tours in nearby Nashik.

This combination of relaxation, fine dining, and adventure is what makes Manas Lifestyle Resort one of the best resorts in Igatpuri for all types of travelers. Whether you’re here for leisure, a celebration, or a corporate offsite, the resort ensures an extraordinary stay.

Best Resorts Near Nashik for Couples – A Romantic Escape in Igatpuri

If you’re planning a romantic getaway, you’ll want a place that feels intimate yet luxurious. Manas Lifestyle Resort is consistently ranked among the best resorts near Nashik for couples. The peaceful setting, mountain views, and personalized experiences make it a favorite choice for honeymooners and couples celebrating special occasions.

Imagine sipping morning tea with your partner on a private balcony overlooking misty valleys, spending the afternoon at the spa, and enjoying a candlelight dinner under the stars. The resort also arranges customized couple experiences such as private bonfire nights, wine-tasting tours at Nashik vineyards, and adventure activities for thrill-seeking duos.

For those looking for privacy, the resort’s villas and cottages provide the perfect escape. With spacious interiors, elegant décor, and complete privacy, it is no surprise that Manas Lifestyle Resort is considered the best resort in Igatpuri for couples seeking luxury and romance.

Best Resort in Nashik for Family – Comfort, Fun & Togetherness

When it comes to planning a family vacation, comfort, safety, and entertainment are top priorities. That’s why Manas Lifestyle Resort is known as the best resort in Nashik for family holidays. The resort offers large suites and interconnected villas that are ideal for families traveling with children and grandparents.

Kids can spend hours enjoying outdoor games, cycling in the resort’s open spaces, or splashing in the swimming pool. Parents appreciate the safe environment, delicious multi-cuisine meals, and attentive staff who ensure every family member feels at home. The resort’s location also allows for easy day trips to nearby attractions such as the Sula Vineyards in Nashik, Tringalwadi Fort, Camel Valley, and Bhavali Dam.

With a perfect balance of relaxation and activities, Manas Lifestyle Resort is one of the top resorts in Igatpuri Nashik for families seeking memorable holidays.

Explore Igatpuri – Attractions Near the Best Resort in Nashik

One of the biggest advantages of staying at Manas Lifestyle Resort is its proximity to some of the best attractions in Nashik and Igatpuri. Guests can explore:

  • Sula Vineyards & Nashik Wine Tours – Just 45 minutes away, perfect for wine lovers.

  • Tringalwadi Fort Trek – A favorite for trekking enthusiasts.

  • Camel Valley & Bhavali Dam – Scenic picnic spots surrounded by waterfalls and greenery.

  • Vipassana International Academy – For meditation and spiritual retreats.

  • Kalsubai Peak – Maharashtra’s highest peak, great for adventure seekers.
