Welcome to Manas Lifestyle Resort – Best Resort in Igatpuri for School Picnics
Looking for a safe, spacious and fun-filled destination for your school picnic? Manas Lifestyle Resort in Igatpuri is a trusted choice for schools across Mumbai, Thane, Nashik and nearby cities. Surrounded by the green Sahyadri hills, our resort gives students a refreshing break from the classroom, with open lawns, a swimming pool, games, delicious food and a supportive team that looks after every detail.
Every year, we host more than 80 school picnics, including many large and well-known schools that visit us with big groups. This experience helps us manage hundreds of students at a time, smoothly and safely, so teachers can relax and children can enjoy a day they will remember.
From the moment your buses arrive to the moment they leave, our team takes care of welcome, seating, activities, meals and supervision support. Whether it is a one-day outing or an overnight trip, we make your school picnic simple to plan and joyful to experience.

FAQs
Q1. What is the minimum and maximum group size for a school picnic?
We host groups of all sizes, from small classes to several hundred students at a time. Share your numbers and we will confirm availability.
Q2. Do you offer one-day and overnight picnics?
Yes. We offer both one-day picnics and overnight trips for schools.
Q3. What meals are included?
Packages usually include breakfast, lunch and evening tea with snacks. Menus are kid-friendly and can be customised.
Q4. Is the resort safe for children?
Yes. We have a secure campus, trained staff and supervised activity areas. Teachers stay in charge of their students, and our team supports them throughout.
Q5. How far is Manas Lifestyle Resort from Mumbai?
We are located on Mumbai Agra Highway, Talegaon, Igatpuri, Nashik, around 120 km from Mumbai, making it a convenient day-trip destination.
Q6. Is there parking for school buses?
Yes, we have ample parking space for multiple buses.
Q7. How can I book a school picnic?
Call our corporate and group desk on 9850002245 or email sales@manaslifestyleresort.in with your school name, date, group size and requirements.
