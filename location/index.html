Getting here

By road, rail or coach

01

By road from Mumbai

Follow the Mumbai–Nashik highway through Thane and the Shahapur belt, then climb Kasara Ghat. Leaving before 7 am usually means a clear ghat and an early check-in by the pool.

02

By train

Suburban trains from Mumbai end at Kasara; long-distance trains stop at Igatpuri station. Rail from Mumbai takes close to 3 hours. Take a taxi or auto for the last stretch.

03

From Nashik & Pune

Nashik is 40 km away — an easy day trip. From Pune (about 230 km) most families book two nights so the journey is worth it.

Nearby attractions

Places to visit around Igatpuri

Ask the front desk for timings and the best season for each. Our guide to places to visit in Igatpuri and things to do has more.

01

Bhatsa River Valley

Less than 3 km from the resort. Sweeping valley views and a cool breeze — best at sunrise and sunset.

02

Camel Valley

A short distance beyond the Bhatsa valley; a steep slope that drops more than 1,000 ft with waterfalls in the monsoon. Stay behind the railings.

03

Kasara Ghat

The scenic climb on the way from Mumbai — mist and waterfalls during the rains.

04

Vihigaon Waterfall

Roughly 120 ft high and at its best in the monsoon.

05

Vipassana International Academy (Dhamma Giri)

A renowned meditation centre in Igatpuri — please visit quietly.

06

Tringalwadi Fort

A historic hill fort popular with trekkers for its views.

07

Vaitarna Lake

A calm, scenic reservoir for a slow afternoon drive.

08

Kalsubai Peak

The highest peak in the Sahyadri range, about 35 km from Igatpuri — an early-morning trek.

09

Trimbakeshwar Temple

One of the twelve Jyotirlingas, a popular pilgrimage from Igatpuri.

Good to know

Travel questions

How far is Manas Lifestyle Resort from Mumbai?

About 120 km — roughly 2.5 hours by road via the Mumbai–Nashik highway, Thane and Kasara Ghat.

How far is the resort from Nashik?

About 40 km, around 40 minutes by road.

How far is it from Pune?

Roughly 230 km, around 5 hours by road depending on traffic.

Can I reach the resort by train?

Yes. Mumbai suburban trains run to Kasara, and long-distance trains stop at Igatpuri. Take a taxi or auto for the final stretch, and call reservations beforehand so the team can guide your driver.

Is there parking at the resort?

Yes, free parking is available, including space for buses.
