Weekend Getaway in Igatpuri Hills – Your Complete Guide to Stay & Celebrate

If you’re looking for a short escape from the busy city life of Mumbai or Nashik, Igatpuri is the perfect destination. Surrounded by the Sahyadri ranges, this charming hill town is known for its cool climate, lush greenery, and peaceful vibes. Families, couples, and even corporate groups are choosing Igatpuri not just for its natural beauty but also for its modern resorts and event venues.

Among the many options, Manas Lifestyle Resort Igatpuri stands out as a luxury property that blends comfort, hospitality, and convenience. Whether you’re planning a family holiday, a wedding, a corporate retreat, or a weekend with friends, this resort offers everything you need under one roof.

A Perfect Escape from the City

Igatpuri is less than three hours from Mumbai, making it an ideal weekend getaway. Driving through the scenic Kasara Ghat itself feels like a holiday before the holiday begins. Travelers often stop at attractions like:

  • Kasara Ghat View Point – for breathtaking valley views.
  • Ghatandevi Mata Mandir – a spiritual landmark.
  • Railway Tunnel View Point & Railway Bridge – popular with photography lovers.
  • Camel Valley Point – a monsoon favorite with seasonal waterfalls

Located just off the Mumbai–Nashik Expressway, Manas Lifestyle Resort is strategically positioned close to these attractions, making it easy to combine sightseeing with relaxation.

Banquets & Lawns – Spaces that Define Celebrations

When it comes to hosting events, Manas Lifestyle Resort has some of the most versatile spaces in Igatpuri:

  • Mist Ballroom – The largest in Igatpuri, with 4,660 sq. ft. of space, perfect for conferences, grand receptions, and gatherings.
  • Mist I & Mist II – Flexible halls covering 2,330 sq. ft., ideal for mid-sized events.
  • Royal Lawn – Spanning 22,500 sq. ft., accommodating up to 1,200 guests, a dream setting for weddings and large-scale events.
  • Cottage Lawn – A charming 17,400 sq. ft. outdoor venue for receptions and intimate celebrations.

These venues are backed by expert event planning services, ensuring your celebrations are memorable and stress-free.

Corporate Retreats in the Heart of Nature

For companies looking to blend work with leisure, Manas Lifestyle Resort is an excellent choice. The property offers:

  • Venues for annual conferences, day workshops, and residential meetings.
  • Customizable packages to suit team-building activities and corporate goals.
  • Comfortable accommodations with modern amenities.
  • Dining and leisure spaces to balance productivity with relaxation.

The combination of professional facilities and a serene environment makes it one of the most trusted destinations for corporate events near Mumbai.

Weddings that Create Lifetime Memories

Planning a wedding in Igatpuri brings together nature’s beauty and grand celebrations. Manas Lifestyle Resort has been the backdrop for countless weddings, offering:

  • Indoor and outdoor venues to host both intimate and large-scale ceremonies.
  • Dedicated event managers for seamless execution.
  • Luxury accommodation for families and guests.
  • Gourmet dining experiences with multi-cuisine options.

From pre-wedding functions to grand receptions, the resort provides everything needed to make weddings timeless and unforgettable.

Dining that Delights Every Palate

Food is at the heart of every holiday and celebration, and Manas Lifestyle Resort ensures a memorable dining experience with:

  • Baithak – A multi-cuisine restaurant with authentic Indian flavors.
  • Café Fusion – A cheerful all-day dining space, perfect for families and groups.
  • Sweet Poison Bar Lounge – A cozy place for cocktails, mocktails, and conversations.

Guests can indulge in both vegetarian specialties and international cuisines, making dining an experience in itself.
