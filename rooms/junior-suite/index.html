The experience

Inside the Junior Suite

The Junior Suite gives families what they ask us for most: an extra bed, a little outdoor space, and a view of the pool so the day can start the moment the curtains open.

Across 450 sq ft of modern design, the suite combines a double bed and an additional single bed with air conditioning, a television and a mini fridge. The veranda is the heart of it — a shaded spot to watch the pool, dry off between swims or share chai while the children plan their next game.

The bathroom is well equipped with essential toiletries, and complimentary snacks from the bar add a small, welcome indulgence after a long drive up the ghats.
