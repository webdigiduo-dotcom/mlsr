Corporate events: FAQs

Is Manas a good venue for a corporate offsite near Mumbai?

Yes. The resort is about 120 km (around 2.5 hours) from Mumbai and 40 km from Nashik, with conference halls, lawns, rooms and villas, dining and team activities on one campus.

What conference facilities are available?

The Mist Ballroom seats up to 350 theatre-style or 120 boardroom-style, and Mist I and II each seat 100 theatre-style. The resort lists fully equipped conference rooms, projectors, audio systems and high-speed internet.

Can you organise team-building activities?

Yes — cricket turf, pickleball, badminton, football, wooden challenge games, indoor games and group activities can be arranged for teams.

Do you offer residential conference packages?

Yes. Packages can be tailored for day and residential conferences, combining venues, accommodation and meals.

Who do I contact for corporate bookings?

Call the corporate desk on +91 98500 02245 or email sales@manaslifestyleresort.in.
