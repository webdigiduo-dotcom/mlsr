Good to know

Villa stays: good to know

Which villas at Manas have a private pool?

The Royal Villa (4 BHK) and all three Urban Villas (4, 6 and 7 BHK) have their own private swimming pool.

Do the villas have a kitchen?

The Urban Villas have a fully equipped kitchen. All villas list an induction cooktop, fridge and electric kettle among their amenities.

Can I use the resort facilities while staying in a villa?

Yes. Villa guests can enjoy the resort’s restaurants, swimming pool, games and activities, along with daily housekeeping and in-room dining.

Are there larger villas for big groups?

The Manas villa collection also includes Ubud and other villas listed on manaslifestylevillas.com. Call reservations to match a villa to your group size.

Is a villa suitable for a wedding family?

Yes. The 6 and 7 BHK Urban Villas are popular with wedding families who want privacy close to the banquets and lawns.
