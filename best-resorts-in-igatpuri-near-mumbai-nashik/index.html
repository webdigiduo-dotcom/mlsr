Best Resorts in Igatpuri for Luxury, Family & Romantic Stays

Igatpuri has emerged as one of Maharashtra’s most loved hill destinations, attracting travelers from Mumbai, Nashik, and across the state. Surrounded by lush mountains, misty mornings, and serene landscapes, it is the perfect escape from city life. Whether you are planning a family vacation, a romantic getaway, a wedding, or a corporate event, choosing the best resort in Igatpuri makes all the difference. Among the many options available, Manas Lifestyle Resort consistently stands out as one of the best resorts in Igatpuri, offering luxury, comfort, scenic beauty, and unique experiences that are hard to find elsewhere.

Why Igatpuri Is One of the Best Resort Destinations Near Mumbai & Nashik

Located conveniently between Mumbai and Nashik, Igatpuri is known for its cool climate, green hills, and peaceful surroundings. Unlike crowded hill stations, Igatpuri offers calm, space, and natural beauty, making it ideal for weekend breaks as well as longer holidays. This is why travelers searching for a resort in Igatpuri Nashik or a weekend getaway near Mumbai increasingly prefer Igatpuri over other destinations.

When guests look for the best luxury resorts in Igatpuri, they usually consider:
  • Scenic mountain views

  • Family and couple-friendly environment

  • Peaceful surroundings and unique experiences
Manas Lifestyle Resort checks all these boxes — and goes beyond them.
