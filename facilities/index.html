Live well, play more

Experiences & activities at the resort

A pool and rain dance, an arcade, indoor and outdoor games, magic shows and pottery — enough to fill a weekend without once getting in the car.

Signature experiences

Eight reasons the weekend flies by

Resort with indoor games, outdoor sports and a rain dance in Igatpuri — made for families, friends and colleagues alike.

01

Swimming Pool

A generous pool with different depths — easy family swims by day, glowing reflections by night.

Shallow and deeper sections make it easy for every age to swim safely, and the lawns around it are perfect for drying off in the sun. Pair it with a Premium Room or Studio Suite for the shortest walk to the water.

02

Rain Dance

Music, open sky and a rain shower to dance under until you are happily exhausted.

A favourite of families and school groups, the rain dance turns an ordinary afternoon into a party. Slides, swings and — subject to availability — an inflatable balloon add to the fun.

03

Arcade Games

Air hockey, car and bike racing and basketball arcade — classic and modern games for every age.

Challenge friends to air hockey, race cars and bikes, or shoot hoops on the basketball arcade. Arcade games are charged per game.

04

Indoor Games

Table tennis, pool table, chess, carrom and a shelf of board games for lazy afternoons.

When the monsoon sets in, the indoor games room comes alive: table tennis, a pool table (₹100 per game), carrom and chess, plus Ludo, Sequence, Jenga, Monopoly and more.

05

Outdoor Games

Cricket turf, badminton, football and a pickleball court in the fresh hill air.

Book the cricket turf or pickleball court by the hour, or join a friendly game of badminton or football on the lawns. Wooden challenge games like Foot Fight, Coin Combat and Tilt Tower bring out everyone’s competitive side.

06

Magic Shows

Sleight of hand and mind-bending illusions that leave children — and parents — spellbound.

Our magician’s sleight of hand and mind-boggling illusions are a highlight for children — and a surprise hit with the adults.

07

Pottery Making

Sit at the wheel with a skilled instructor and shape something to take home.

A calm, creative hour at the potter’s wheel, guided by skilled instructors. Pot making is ₹100 per pot.

08

EV Charging

An on-site electric-vehicle charging station, so you can arrive and leave fully charged.

Driving electric from Mumbai or Nashik? Charge on site while you enjoy your stay.

Good to know

Activities: what guests ask

Does Manas Lifestyle Resort have a swimming pool?

Yes — a swimming pool with different depths for families, along with a rain dance area, slides and swings.

What indoor games are available?

Table tennis, a pool table, chess, carrom, Ludo, Snakes & Ladders, Sequence, Jenga, Monopoly and playing cards, plus an arcade with air hockey, car racing, bike racing and basketball.

What outdoor activities can we do?

Cricket turf, cricket, badminton, football and a pickleball court, plus wooden challenge games such as Foot Fight, Coin Combat and Tilt Tower.

Are activities free?

Many are included. The cricket turf and pickleball court are charged by the hour, arcade games per game, the pool table at ₹100 per game and pot making at ₹100 per pot. Please confirm current timings and charges with the resort.

Is there an EV charging station?

Yes, there is an electric-vehicle charging station on site for guests.

Is the resort good for kids?

Very. Between the pool, rain dance, arcade, games, magic shows and pottery, children have plenty to do — and parents get to relax.
