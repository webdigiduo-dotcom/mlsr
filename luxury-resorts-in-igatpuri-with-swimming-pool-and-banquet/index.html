Luxury Resorts in Igatpuri with Swimming Pool, Indoor–Outdoor Games & Banquet Venues – A Verified Travel Guide

About Manas Lifestyle Resort and Location

Manas Lifestyle Resort is located at 286A, Talegaon, Igatpuri, Maharashtra 422403.
It lies approximately 120 km from Mumbai and 45 km from Nashik, connected by NH 160 (Mumbai–Nashik Highway).
The Igatpuri Railway Station is around 2 km away.
Verified on major travel platforms, the resort is rated 8.1 / 10 on Booking.com and 4.2 / 5 on Google, recognized for its range of facilities and convenient location near the Western Ghats.
For general property details, visit Resorts in Igatpuri.

Accommodation and Amenities

The resort offers Deluxe Rooms, Villas and Suites, as listed on its All Accommodations page. All units include air-conditioning, private bathrooms, televisions, Wi-Fi, and comfortable bedding. Select rooms provide balconies with mountain or garden views. Daily housekeeping and room service are available for guests.

Swimming Pool and Rain Shower Area

An outdoor swimming pool with different depth zones is available for both adults and children. Guests can also enjoy a rain shower area designed for fun and relaxation. These facilities are part of the verified Facilities listed on the official site and OTAs. Pool access is limited to in-house guests during operational hours.

Indoor and Outdoor Games

Guests can enjoy several recreational activities without leaving the property. Indoor games include table tennis, snooker, and a dedicated arcade gaming zone. Outdoor activities include cricket matches, badminton, yoga, and picnic spaces in open lawns. These options are highlighted on the resort’s Facilities page to ensure a complete family-friendly experience.

Unique Guest Experiences

Manas Lifestyle Resort provides more than just rooms and food.
Guests can attend magic shows, participate in pot-making sessions guided by instructors, and use the on-site electric vehicle (EV) charging station, which supports sustainable travel.
All three activities are confirmed features on the official website’s Facilities section.

Destination Weddings in Igatpuri

According to the Igatpuri Resorts for Wedding page, the resort manages complete wedding arrangements.
Venue options include Mist Ballroom, Royal Lawn, and Cottage Lawn, supporting events of 700 – 1,200 guests.
Dining options for wedding functions include Jain, Gujarati, Rajasthani and International menus prepared by the in-house culinary team.
The scenic hill views around the property serve as natural photography backdrops.

Corporate Events and Conferences

Manas Lifestyle Resort hosts a wide range of corporate programs such as annual meets, seminars, doctor conferences, educational tours, and staff outings.
Facilities include audio-visual equipment, high-speed internet, banquet spaces, and customizable packages.
Details are listed on the Resorts for Corporate Outing Near Mumbai.
The resort’s location on NH 160 provides easy access for corporate travelers from Mumbai and Pune.

Dining Options at the Resort

Dining facilities are outlined on the Multi-Cuisine Restaurant in Igatpuri page:

  • Baithak – à la carte Indian restaurant serving regional specialties.
  • Café Fusion – all-day buffet restaurant for breakfast, lunch and dinner.
  • Sweet Poison Bar Lounge – serves mocktails and cocktails in a lounge setting.

All three outlets offer vegetarian and multi-cuisine menus for families, couples and corporate guests.

Family-Friendly Facilities

The property features large lawns, a kids’ play area, a family-safe pool zone and multiple indoor games.
Activities like magic shows and pot-making are suited for all age groups.
For families planning weekend stays, verified information is available on the Best Resort in Igatpuri for Family.

Guests can book directly through the official website or verified OTAs such as Booking.com, Agoda, and MakeMyTrip. Current discounts and package deals are updated on the Offers page. Check-in time is 1:00 PM, and check-out is 11:00 AM, as listed on OTA portals.

FAQs

Q1. Does Manas Lifestyle Resort have a swimming pool?

Yes, the resort offers one outdoor pool with family-friendly depth zones.

Q2. Are indoor and outdoor games available?

Yes, guests can enjoy table tennis, snooker, arcade games, cricket and badminton.

Q3. What are the restaurant options on site?

Baithak, Café Fusion and Sweet Poison Bar Lounge serve Indian and multi-cuisine menus.

Q4. Can the resort host weddings and corporate events?

Yes, venues like the Mist Ballroom and Royal Lawn accommodate up to 1,200 guests for weddings and conferences.

Q5. Is EV charging available on site?

Yes, an electric vehicle charging station is provided for guests.

Summary

Manas Lifestyle Resort Igatpuri offers verified facilities including an outdoor swimming pool, indoor and outdoor games, multiple banquet venues, three dining options, and EV charging stations.
Located 120 km from Mumbai and 45 km from Nashik, it is suited for family holidays, corporate events and destination weddings.
