Mango Festival 2026 in Igatpuri at Manas Lifestyle Resort – A Sweet Summer Getaway

As summer arrives, the season of vacations, travel, and sweet treats begins. Exams are over, bags are getting packed, and families are planning refreshing getaways. And what makes summer even more special? The king of fruits — Mangoes. Their juicy sweetness, delicious aroma, and the joy of sharing them with loved ones make this season truly unforgettable.

Now imagine enjoying all of this in one place — the excitement of a holiday and the irresistible taste of fresh mangoes.

That’s exactly what you’ll experience at the “Mango Festival-The Kings Feast” at Manas Lifestyle Resort.

It’s a celebration where summer fun meets the sweetness of mangoes. Relax, taste different mango varieties, enjoy delicious treats, and spend quality time with your loved ones.

Celebrate the Mango Festival in the Heart of Igatpuri

Known for its lush green hills, pleasant climate, and peaceful atmosphere, Igatpuri is one of Maharashtra’s most popular hill destinations. During the summer season, this beautiful hill station becomes even more vibrant as visitors gather to enjoy the much-anticipated Mango Festival.

The Mango Festival at Manas Lifestyle Resort celebrates the rich flavors and versatility of mangoes. Guests can experience a variety of mango-based dishes, desserts, beverages, and seasonal specialties crafted by expert chefs at the resort.

The event is designed not just as a food celebration but as a complete summer experience where visitors can relax, explore, and indulge in the sweet taste of mangoes while enjoying nature.

Mango Festival Menu – “The Kings Feast” Special at Manas Lifestyle Resort

One of the most exciting highlights of the Mango Festival starting 1st April 2026 at Manas Lifestyle Resort is the specially curated Mango Festival Menu called “The Kings Feast.” This exclusive menu celebrates the rich taste of mangoes through creative drinks, refreshing salads, flavorful starters, delicious mains, and indulgent desserts.

Guests visiting the Mango Festival in Igatpuri can enjoy a variety of unique mango-inspired dishes prepared by the resort’s expert chefs. From traditional favorites like Aamras Puri to innovative dishes like Mango Salsa Tacos, the menu is designed to offer a complete culinary journey for mango lovers.

Why Igatpuri is the Perfect Destination for a Mango Festival

Igatpuri is known for its natural beauty and pleasant climate throughout the year. Located in the Western Ghats of Maharashtra, it offers stunning landscapes, fresh mountain air, and a calm atmosphere.

Here are some reasons why Igatpuri is the perfect destination to celebrate the Mango Festival:

Beautiful natural surroundings
The hills, greenery, and scenic views make it a refreshing place to enjoy summer.

Close to major cities
The destination is easily accessible from Mumbai and Nashik, making it ideal for weekend trips.

Peaceful getaway
Visitors can relax away from the busy city lifestyle while enjoying nature.

Unique seasonal event
The Mango Festival adds a fun and flavorful element to a summer vacation.

Combining travel, relaxation, and food, the festival offers a well-rounded experience for visitors.

From delicious mango dishes to exciting activities and relaxing stays, the festival offers something for everyone. Whether you visit with family, friends, or colleagues, the event promises a joyful celebration of the king of fruits.

The Mango Festival is the perfect opportunity to enjoy the sweetness of mangoes while spending quality time in the serene surroundings of Igatpuri.

Seats and stays during the festival fill up quickly, so plan your visit in advance and enjoy a refreshing summer escape filled with delicious mango dishes, exciting activities, and relaxing moments in the beautiful hills of Igatpuri.
