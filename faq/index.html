Frequently asked questions

FAQs — Manas Lifestyle Resort, Igatpuri

Everything guests ask about stays, villas, dining, weddings and corporate events near Mumbai and Nashik.

Good to know

Location & getting here

Where is Manas Lifestyle Resort located?

At 286-A, Mumbai–Agra Highway, Talegaon, Igatpuri, Nashik district, Maharashtra 422403 — a convenient weekend getaway from Mumbai and Nashik.

How far is the resort from Mumbai and Nashik?

About 120 km (roughly 2.5 hours) from Mumbai and 40 km (about 40 minutes) from Nashik. See directions.

Is there parking available?

Yes, ample free parking, including space for buses — convenient for weddings, corporate events and family groups.

What is the best time to visit?

The resort is lovely all year. The monsoon is special, when mist and fog drift across the hills and lawns; winter brings cool, clear days.

Which tourist attractions are nearby?

Bhatsa River Valley, Camel Valley, Kasara Ghat, Vihigaon Waterfall, the Vipassana International Academy, Tringalwadi Fort, Kalsubai Peak, Trimbakeshwar Temple and Sandhan Valley.

Good to know

Rooms & villas

What types of rooms and villas are available?

Luxury Cottages, Premium, Urban and Luxe Rooms, Junior, Studio, Signature and Harmony Suites, a 650 sq ft Suite, and private pool villas: Royal Villa 4 BHK and Urban Villas 4, 6 and 7 BHK. Compare all stays.

Do you have luxury villas with private pools?

Yes. The Royal Villa and every Urban Villa have a private swimming pool. The wider Manas villa collection, including Ubud villas, is listed on manaslifestylevillas.com.

Are the villas suitable for long stays?

Yes. Villas have living areas and kitchen facilities (fully equipped kitchens in the Urban Villas), making them comfortable for extended family holidays and retreats.

Can I book a villa for a family gathering or a weekend with friends?

Yes — the 4, 6 and 7 BHK villas are designed for exactly that. See the villas.

Is the resort couple-friendly?

Absolutely. The Luxury Cottage, Signature Suite and Harmony Suite are favourites with couples, and our team can arrange romantic dinners, proposals and surprise décor.

Good to know

Families & activities

Is Manas Lifestyle Resort family-friendly?

Yes. Families enjoy the pool, rain dance, lawns, arcade, indoor and outdoor games, magic shows and pottery, plus rooms with an extra bed.

Does the resort have a swimming pool?

Yes — a well-maintained pool with different depths, plus a rain dance area.

What activities can guests enjoy?

Swimming, rain dance, arcade games, table tennis, pool table, carrom and board games, cricket turf, badminton, football, pickleball, wooden challenge games, magic shows, pottery and, subject to availability, DJ or live band nights. Full list.

Are activities chargeable?

Most are included. The cricket turf and pickleball court are charged hourly, arcade games per game, pool table ₹100 per game and pot making ₹100 per pot.

Do you have an EV charging station?

Yes, there is an EV charging station for guests.

Is the resort pet-friendly?

We love animals, but pets are not permitted inside the resort, to maintain hygiene and comfort for all guests.

Do you host birthdays and anniversaries?

Yes. Our Birthday & Anniversary package decorates your room with balloons, rose petals, towel art and a cake. See offers.

Good to know

Dining

What dining options are available?

Baithak (Indian à la carte), Café Fusion (all-day dining with buffets), Sweet Poison (bar lounge) and in-room dining.

Is outside food allowed?

No, outside food and beverages are not permitted. Our restaurants serve a wide variety of freshly prepared meals.

Do you arrange special dining experiences?

Yes — candle-lit dinners, poolside dining and customised menus for celebrations, weddings and corporate groups.

Good to know

Weddings & events

Does the resort host weddings?

Yes. Manas offers banquet halls, lawns and customisable packages for weddings in Igatpuri. Weddings at Manas.

How many guests can the banquet halls and lawns accommodate?

The Mist Ballroom hosts up to 500 guests reception-style, the Cottage Lawn up to 700 and the Royal Lawn up to 1,200. Compare venues.

Do you offer destination wedding packages?

Yes, covering décor, catering, accommodation and event planning support.

Can small weddings or intimate ceremonies be hosted?

Yes, in Mist I or Mist II or on the lawns.

Do you host pre-wedding functions?

Yes — Mehendi, Sangeet, Haldi and engagement ceremonies.

Can corporate events and conferences be hosted?

Yes — day and residential conferences, doctor conferences, seminars, annual events and staff outings. Corporate events.

Are team-building activities available?

Yes, including outdoor sports, indoor games and group activities.
