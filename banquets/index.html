Good to know

Venue questions

What is the largest banquet hall at Manas?

The Mist Ballroom: 4,660 sq ft (88′ × 53′ × 13′), seating 350 theatre-style or 500 for a reception.

How many guests can the lawns hold?

The Royal Lawn (22,500 sq ft) holds up to 1,200 for a reception and the Cottage Lawn (17,400 sq ft) up to 700.

Can the ballroom be divided?

Yes. Mist I and Mist II are 2,330 sq ft each and seat 100 theatre-style or 200 for a reception.

Which events can I host?

Weddings and receptions, engagements, birthdays and anniversaries, conferences, seminars, training programmes and corporate celebrations.

Is there parking for guests and buses?

Yes, ample free parking is available, including space for buses.
