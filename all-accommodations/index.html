Good to know

Choosing your room

Which rooms are best for families?

The Premium Room, Junior Suite and Studio Suite each have a double bed plus an additional single bed, and the 650 sq ft Suite adds a separate living room. Larger families and groups usually book a private pool villa.

Which stay is best for couples?

The Luxury Cottage (with a private sit-out), the Signature Suite (with an in-room bathtub with a view) and the Harmony Suite (king bed and bathtub) are the most popular with couples.

Do you have villas with a private pool in Igatpuri?

Yes. The Royal Villa (4 BHK) and the Urban Villas (4, 6 and 7 BHK) each have a private swimming pool. See all villas.

What amenities do all rooms have?

Every room, suite and villa includes air conditioning, daily housekeeping, an electric kettle, in-room dining, in-room Wi-Fi and a television.

Are pets or outside food allowed?

Pets are not permitted, and outside food and beverages are not allowed. Our restaurants and in-room dining serve meals throughout the day.
