Best Resort Near Mumbai for a Perfect Weekend Getaway – Manas Lifestyle Resort

Tired of the city rush and looking for a refreshing escape? A short drive away from the bustling streets of Mumbai lies the charming hill station of Igatpuri, surrounded by misty mountains, waterfalls, and lush greenery. Whether you’re planning a family vacation, a romantic retreat, or a corporate outing, finding the best resort near Mumbai is all about comfort, accessibility, and unforgettable experiences — and that’s where Manas Lifestyle Resort stands out.

Igatpuri – The Ideal Weekend Getaway Near Mumbai

Located just around 120 km from Mumbai, Igatpuri is one of Maharashtra’s most scenic hill stations. Its cool climate, serene surroundings, and easy connectivity via the Mumbai–Nashik Highway make it a favorite destination for travelers seeking a quick break from city life.

What makes Igatpuri unique is its peaceful atmosphere — perfect for families, couples, and friends looking for a quiet escape surrounded by nature. Over the years, it has also become a hub for luxury resorts near Mumbai, offering both relaxation and recreation in one destination.

Manas Lifestyle Resort – Luxury in the Heart of Igatpuri

Nestled amid the hills, Manas Lifestyle Resort offers a perfect blend of modern luxury and natural beauty. Spread across acres of greenery, the resort provides a peaceful ambiance that allows you to unwind and reconnect with yourself and your loved ones.

Whether it’s the elegant rooms, scenic views, or warm hospitality, Manas Lifestyle Resort redefines what a luxury resort near Mumbai should feel like — calm, comfortable, and connected to nature.

Easy to Reach, Hard to Leave

Manas Lifestyle Resort is just a 2.5-hour drive from Mumbai and around 1 hour from Nashik, located conveniently on the Mumbai–Nashik Highway. The scenic route lined with hills and lakes makes the journey as enjoyable as the destination itself.

Its close proximity to both cities makes it one of the most convenient hill station resorts near Mumbai for spontaneous weekend getaways or quick retreats.
