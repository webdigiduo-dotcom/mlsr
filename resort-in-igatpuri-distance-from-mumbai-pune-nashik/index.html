Resort in Igatpuri Near Mumbai, Pune and Nashik: Distance and Travel Time Guide

Manas Lifestyle Resort is a resort in Igatpuri on the Mumbai–Agra Highway in Talegaon, about 120 km and 2.5 hours from Mumbai, and about 40 km (40 minutes) from Nashik. Pune is roughly 230 km away, around 5 hours by road. Thane and Kalyan fall in between, which is why many families treat it as a one-night escape rather than a full holiday. This guide lists the distance and travel time from each major starting point, the best way to reach the resort by road, train or cab, and the sights you will pass on the way. Times are approximate and change with traffic and weather.

Resort in Igatpuri Near Mumbai: Distance and Drive Time From Mumbai, Borivali and Thane

The resort is about 120 km from Mumbai and the drive takes roughly 2.5 hours. The route follows the Mumbai–Nashik highway through Thane and the Shahapur belt, then climbs Kasara Ghat before reaching Igatpuri. Guests starting from the western suburbs such as Malad or Borivali should expect about 125 km, and about 2.5 to 3 hours if traffic is heavy around Thane.

From Thane the distance is close to 100 km and from Kalyan about 90 km, so the drive is usually a little over 2 hours. Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport is around 140 km away, so plan on roughly 3 hours by taxi.

Leaving before 7 a.m. helps you cross Kasara Ghat in clear light and reach check-in with time for the pool. If you prefer a shorter hop, see the weekend getaway resort near Mumbai guide, or compare the best resort near Mumbai for family and couple stays.

Resort in Igatpuri Near Pune: Distance and Travel Time From Pune

Pune is roughly 230 km from Igatpuri. Intercity cab estimates put the road journey at around 5 hours, though this depends on the route and traffic through the Mumbai side of the ghats. The resort is a little before Igatpuri town if you are arriving from Mumbai, so ask your driver to navigate to Talegaon on the Mumbai–Agra Highway.

By train, most travellers take a Pune to Kalyan service and then continue to Kasara or Igatpuri. Allow 5 to 7 hours in total, including the change at Kalyan, and book a taxi for the last stretch because there is no resort shuttle listed.

Because the trip is long, families from Pune usually book two nights. That leaves a full day for the pool, games and nearby sights instead of spending the whole stay in the car. Larger groups can compare villas in Igatpuri that range from 2BHK to 15BHK, and free parking includes space for buses.

Resort in Igatpuri Near Nashik: Distance and Travel Time From Nashik and Kasara

Nashik is the closest major city. The resort is about 40 km from Nashik, a drive of around 40 minutes, and the town of Igatpuri sits roughly 45 km from the city centre. That makes it an easy choice for a same-day outing, a family lunch or a corporate off-site from Nashik.

On the Mumbai side, Kasara is about 20 km from Igatpuri town, and the ghat section between the two is where the road climbs the Western Ghats. Local trains stop at Kasara, so some guests from Mumbai take a train to Kasara and then a taxi uphill.

If you live in Nashik and want to compare options on your side of the district, read about the best resort near Nashik. For travellers coming from either city, the guide to the best resorts in Igatpuri near Mumbai and Nashik shows how the two routes compare.

Resort in Igatpuri by Train, Bus and Cab: How to Reach Without a Car

Suburban trains from Mumbai end at Kasara, while long-distance trains stop at Igatpuri railway station, so you can pick the option that suits your schedule. A direct journey by rail from Mumbai takes close to 3 hours, and from Thane about 2 to 3 hours, depending on the train.

From either station, a taxi or auto is the simplest way to cover the final stretch. Share the pickup point and arrival time with the team beforehand by calling 9850002234 so they can guide your driver. Buses on the Mumbai–Nashik highway also stop at Igatpuri, and drivers know the resort as a landmark on the highway.

For group trips, a hired coach is easier than several cars, and the resort provides free parking with room for buses. Check what the stay includes by opening the resort offers in Igatpuri before you book, and confirm pickup details when you call.

Resort in Igatpuri Nearby Places: What You Can Visit Along the Way

Several sights sit close to the resort. The Bhatsa River Valley lies less than 3 km away, and Camel Valley follows a short distance ahead, where a steep slope drops more than 1,000 ft. Keep behind the railings for photos.

Kasara Ghat is the scenic drive you cross on the way from Mumbai, with mist and waterfalls in the rains. Vihigaon Waterfall, about 120 ft high, is best in the monsoon. The Vipassana International Academy at Dhamma Giri is a meditation centre, so visit quietly. Kalsubai Peak, the highest peak in the Sahyadri range, is around 35 km from Igatpuri and suits early-morning trekkers.

During the monsoon, fog is visible from outside the villas at the resort, which makes mornings on the lawns especially atmospheric. For a full list of stops with timings, use the places to visit in Igatpuri guide and the things to do in Igatpuri travel guide.
