Discover the Best Resorts in Igatpuri for Luxury, Family Stays & Weekend Getaways Near Mumbai

Tucked away in the Sahyadri hills, Igatpuri is one of Maharashtra’s most scenic hill stations — a refreshing escape from the rush of city life. Just a few hours’ drive from Mumbai and Nashik, this charming destination has become the perfect spot for weekend getaways, family vacations, and luxury retreats. Amid the misty mountains and lush valleys, you’ll find some of the best resorts in Igatpuri that offer both tranquility and indulgence — and one name that stands out among them is Manas Lifestyle Resort.

Igatpuri – The Perfect Mix of Peace and Luxury

Igatpuri is more than a hill station — it’s a complete mood. With its cool climate, breathtaking views, and peaceful surroundings, it has become a popular choice for travelers looking for a break from the ordinary. What makes the resorts here special is their balance of nature and comfort. Guests can wake up to mist-covered hills, enjoy world-class amenities, and end the day with a beautiful sunset by the pool.
When people search for the best resorts in Igatpuri, they’re not just looking for a stay — they’re looking for an experience that refreshes both body and mind.

What Makes a Resort in Igatpuri Truly the Best

A great resort isn’t defined by luxury alone; it’s about how well it blends comfort, nature, and service. The best resorts in Igatpuri offer:

  • Spacious accommodations with mountain views
  • A peaceful atmosphere surrounded by greenery
  • A swimming pool for relaxation
  • A multi-cuisine restaurant serving authentic flavors
  • Spaces for weddings, celebrations, or corporate events
  • Warm hospitality that makes every guest feel at home

This is exactly where Manas Lifestyle Resort stands apart.

Manas Lifestyle Resort – Where Nature Meets Luxury

Located in the heart of Igatpuri, Manas Lifestyle Resort is among the top luxury resorts in Igatpuri that offer a mix of elegance, comfort, and scenic beauty. Spread across acres of landscaped greenery, the resort gives guests a true sense of space and serenity.
Every corner is designed to reflect modern luxury while staying rooted in nature. The soothing mountain breeze, open gardens, and charming pathways create the perfect environment for guests to unwind. Whether it’s a family trip, romantic stay, or corporate outing, Manas offers a memorable escape from city life.
