Taste the extraordinary

Multi-cuisine dining in Igatpuri

Three distinct spaces — Baithak, Café Fusion and the Sweet Poison bar lounge — plus in-room dining, all from one generous kitchen.

Food is the key to the heart

Bold flavours, warm interiors, unhurried meals.

From sumptuous vegetarian fare to international favourites, dining at Manas is designed around every palate — and around the long, happy meals that make a holiday.

Our chefs cook Indian, Maharashtrian, Malvani, continental and Asian dishes, and for weddings and events they build menus around Jain, Gujarati and Rajasthani traditions too. Celebrating? Ask about candle-lit dinners, poolside dining and customised menus.

Indian · À la carte

Baithak

Inspired by the traditional gathering it is named after, Baithak is warm, welcoming and proudly Indian. The à la carte menu travels the country — rich North Indian curries, Maharashtrian classics and coastal Malvani flavours — each dish prepared with care and authenticity.

Guests often mention the Chicken Manchow soup, biryani, Burmese khow suey and, in season, the aamras pani puri. In the monsoon, a window table with the hills disappearing into the mist is the best seat in Igatpuri.

All-day dining · Buffets

Café Fusion

Bright, cheerful and home-style, Café Fusion is where the resort gathers for breakfast, lunch and dinner buffets. Families, friends and colleagues fill their plates from a main-course spread, a salad bar and the dessert counters our guests rave about.

Prefer something specific? The kitchen is happy to accommodate chef-crafted preferences — and yes, some tables have swings for chairs.

Bar lounge

Sweet Poison

Pre-dinner drinks, post-dinner conversations and quiet celebrations: the Sweet Poison bar lounge is made for unwinding. Choose from cocktails and mocktails in a relaxed, versatile space indoors and on the sit-out.

Ask the bar team about seasonal specials — the mango mocktail is a guest favourite in summer.

Good to know

Dining at Manas

What restaurants are there at Manas Lifestyle Resort?

Baithak (à la carte Indian cuisine), Café Fusion (all-day dining with breakfast, lunch and dinner buffets) and Sweet Poison (bar lounge with mocktails and cocktails). In-room dining is also available.

Is Manas a multi-cuisine restaurant in Igatpuri?

Yes. Guests enjoy Indian — including Maharashtrian and Malvani dishes — alongside continental, Chinese and Thai favourites, according to guest reviews and our menus.

Can non-resident guests dine at the restaurant?

Please call the resort on +91 98500 02234 to check availability for walk-in or group dining.

Do you cater for weddings and corporate events?

Yes. Our chefs plan menus for weddings, receptions and conferences, including Jain, Gujarati and Rajasthani spreads and international cuisine.

Is outside food allowed?

Outside food and beverages are not permitted at the resort.

Group dining & events

Planning a meal for a group?

Birthdays, family lunches, corporate dinners and celebrations — tell us the occasion and the number of guests.
