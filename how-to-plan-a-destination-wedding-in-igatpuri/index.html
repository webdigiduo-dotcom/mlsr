How to Plan a Perfect Destination Wedding at Manas Lifestyle Resort, Igatpuri

Introduction

Planning a destination wedding in Igatpuri means blending scenic views, cool weather, and warm hospitality. Manas Lifestyle Resort, one of the most beautiful Igatpuri resorts for wedding, provides the perfect venue where celebration meets comfort. Spread across lush green surroundings with banquet halls, lawns, and engaging facilities, this resort offers everything you need for a memorable wedding getaway. Here’s a factual, step-by-step guide to help you plan your dream wedding at Manas Lifestyle Resort, Igatpuri.

1. Select the Ideal Venue for Your Wedding Ceremonies

Manas Lifestyle Resort offers both indoor and outdoor wedding spaces, making it suitable for every season and celebration style.
  • The banquet hall is elegant and fully equipped for grand ceremonies, receptions, or sangeet nights — accommodating up to 400 guests comfortably.
  • The outdoor lawns surrounded by hills offer a naturally scenic setting for pheras, mehndi, or cocktail evenings.
Because the property includes both settings, couples can choose between open-air charm and indoor comfort — or combine both for a multi-day wedding experience.

2. Plan Wedding Décor that Complements the Resort’s Natural Beauty

The resort’s architecture and landscape naturally enhance wedding décor. You can highlight floral mandaps, fairy-light pathways, and thematic backdrops without overpowering the natural surroundings.
Since the lawns are green and expansive, minimalist or royal décor themes both work well. The banquet interiors are neutral-toned, making them ideal for traditional, modern, or fusion setups.
You can coordinate with decorators to design smooth transitions between functions like haldi by the lawn, sangeet in the banquet, and reception dinner under the stars.

3. Customize Food and Beverage Arrangements

Manas Lifestyle Resort has an in-house dining and catering team offering multi-cuisine food options. From traditional Indian delicacies to continental dishes, the resort’s chefs curate menus to match your preferences.
Their catering services handle everything from starters to desserts and beverages, ensuring a flawless dining experience for every wedding event. Couples can also plan themed counters, live food stations, and custom beverages to give guests a rich culinary experience throughout the celebration.

4. Arrange Comfortable Accommodation for Guests

The resort offers a range of accommodation options suitable for all types of guests:
  • Luxury Cottages — perfect for close family and friends.
  • Premium Rooms — equipped with air conditioning, TV, and modern comforts.
  • 6- and 7-Bedroom Villas — ideal for large families or group stays.
Every room includes cozy interiors and direct access to open spaces, ensuring convenience during multi-day celebrations. Since all rooms are within the resort, guests can enjoy functions, rest, and entertainment without any travel.

5. Engage Guests with Fun Pre-Wedding Activities

One of the highlights of Manas Lifestyle Resort is the range of activities and facilities that keep guests entertained between ceremonies:
  • Indoor Games: Table tennis, snooker, and board games help guests relax and bond.
  • Outdoor Games: Cricket, badminton, and lawn games make mornings lively.
  • Arcade Games: A dedicated section with both traditional and modern games adds fun for kids and adults.
  • Magic Show: Live magic performances that delight both families and children.
  • Pot Making: Guests can experience pottery guided by an instructor — a unique and creative addition to wedding festivities.
  • Rain Shower: A fun space where guests can enjoy dancing and rain effects, perfect for light moments before or after functions.
  • Swimming Pool: The resort’s pool area provides a refreshing escape for families and couples between wedding events.
  • EV Charging Station: An eco-friendly facility for guests arriving in electric vehicles.
These facilities turn your destination wedding into a full celebration experience for everyone staying at the resort.

6. Coordinate Smoothly with the Resort’s Event Team

The resort’s experienced wedding and event coordination team helps with every stage of your celebration — from booking venues to guest management.
They assist in:

  • Seating layout planning
  • Vendor coordination
  • Guest check-ins
  • Food and décor arrangements

Their attention to detail ensures all your ceremonies flow seamlessly across multiple days. You can contact the wedding department at 9850002247 for detailed assistance and customized event planning.

Conclusion

Hosting your big day at Manas Lifestyle Resort, Igatpuri means combining nature, elegance, and comfort in one destination. With beautiful lawns, banquet halls, luxurious accommodations, and a range of recreational facilities, it’s an ideal choice among Igatpuri resorts for wedding celebrations.
Every service — from décor and catering to fun guest activities — is managed with care, allowing you to enjoy every moment stress-free. For a wedding that feels personal, joyful, and picture-perfect, Manas Lifestyle Resort makes it truly happen.
